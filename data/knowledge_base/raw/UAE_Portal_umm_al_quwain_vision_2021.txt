Umm Al Quwain Vision 2021

Source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/strategies-plans-and-visions-untill-2021/umm-al-quwain-vision-2021

Launched in 2018, Umm Al Quwain Vision 2021 focuses on providing high-quality living standards for a cohesive society. The vision is based on five main pillars which are:
a sustainable and diversified economy
social solidarity
an attractive tourism
an excellent infrastructure
an innovative government.
In accordance with the vision, Government of Umm Al Quwain aims to implement comprehensive structural development through programmes and initiatives which support:
the growth of the local industries, partnerships with the private sector and an economy based on knowledge, research and development
an environment which nourishes community engagement, volunteerism, community cohesion and familial stability
an industry which attracts tourism and investments, making Umm Al Quwain a top destination for environmental and cultural tourism
a distinguished comprehensive infrastructure in terms of planning, sustainability of assets and quality of life
customers’ happiness and satisfaction by offering excellent comprehensive services and accelerating transformation towards eGovernment.
