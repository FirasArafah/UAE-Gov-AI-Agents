Abu Dhabi Family Wellbeing Strategy

Source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/social-affairs/abu-dhabi-family-wellbeing-strategy

The Abu Dhabi Family Wellbeing Strategy is a comprehensive three-year plan designed to enhance the strength of families, which serve as the fundamental building blocks of a connected, cohesive and productive society.
The strategy includes 30 initiatives; all of which aim to support families in various areas, ranging from fostering financial resilience to providing assistance to parents and empowering senior citizens. The initiatives include:
providing training on financial literacy
preparing couples to form a new family,
introducing work-life balance policies and life skills programmes and
creating community centres and policies to support the wellbeing of seniors.
Department of Community Development – Abu Dhabi launched the strategy, which will be implemented by local and federal entities from across the public, private and social sectors.
