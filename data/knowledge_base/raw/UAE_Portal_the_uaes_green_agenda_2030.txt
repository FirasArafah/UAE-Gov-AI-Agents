The UAE's Green Agenda 2030
The UAE’s Green Agenda - 2030 is a long-term plan to achieve the goals of sustainable development in the UAE and make its economy more environment-friendly. It has strategic objectives and includes various programmes and initiatives to achieve them.
Objectives
The agenda has the following five objectives:
competitive knowledge economy
social development and quality of life
sustainable environment and valued natural resources
clean energy and climate action
green life and sustainable use of resources.
By 2030, the UAE Green Agenda aims to achieve the following:
increase the UAE’s GDP by 4 to 5 percent
increase its exports by about AED 24 to 25 billion
reduce the country’s emissions to less than 100 kilowatt-hours.
