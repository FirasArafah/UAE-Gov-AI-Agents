The General Framework for Adopting Sustainable Digital Transformation
The General Framework for Adopting Sustainable Digital Transformation was issued as a guiding policy to integrate sustainability principles into all activities and initiatives of digital transformation within government entities in the UAE. This strategic step aims to enhance sustainable digital readiness in the UAE.
Objectives
The objectives of the policy are to:
implement the principles of digital sustainability across all digital transformation initiatives and activities to enhance governmental digital readiness and excellence, and
support the UAE’s sustainable development efforts and establish a global benchmark in digital sustainability.
Priorities and key components
The framework includes guiding principles as priorities across 8 pillars to guide public entities in achieving a balance between technological advancement, environmental protection, and a sustainable digital future. These pillars encompass initiatives such as:
the paperless government
cashless government project
green data centers and cloud computing
sustainable digital procurement
green websites, applications and software
sustainable management of digital devices
sustainable digital transformation skills, and
sustainable digital collaboration tools.
Expected outcome
The policy is expected to achieve the following outcome:
enhancing the UAE's global standing in sustainability and sustainable digital transformation
contributing to mitigating the impact of climate change and achieving climate neutrality for the UAE
strengthening government efficiency through sustainable public digital transformation
reducing and managing the environmental impact of government digital transformation initiatives.
