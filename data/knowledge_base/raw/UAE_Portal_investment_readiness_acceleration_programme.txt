‘Investment Readiness Acceleration' programme

Source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/finance-and-economy/investment-readiness-acceleration-programme

The ‘Investment Readiness Acceleration' programme aims to empower women-led start-ups in the UAE by providing them with essential skills to navigate new sectors, secure funding and communicate their vision effectively to potential investors.
This programme is a component of the Entrepreneurial Nation project initiated by Ministry of Economy and it aligns with the UAE's endeavours to advance gender equality and to promote women's participation across diverse economic sectors. It aims to inspire women to establish entrepreneurial ventures that contribute to the economic progress of the UAE.
Launched jointly by Ministry of Economy and the European Women Association (EWA), the programme aims to provide opportunities for women to create projects that foster sustainable economic growth inside and outside the UAE.
