Cyber Security Information Sharing Framework
The Cyber Security Information Sharing Framework enables near real-time sharing of cyber security information among UAE stakeholders. It establishes principles, roles, and methods to enhance collaboration, improve resilience, and support swift responses to cyber threats.
The Cyber Security Council has developed this framework to establish a National Cybersecurity Information Sharing Framework to promote and institutionalise cybersecurity information sharing and collaboration across multiple stakeholders. This framework is aligned with the UAE’s national priority to be a global leader in cyber security and will enhance the security posture of organisations and individuals across the UAE.
