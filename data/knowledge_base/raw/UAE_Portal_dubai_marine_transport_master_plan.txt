Dubai Marine Transport Master Plan-2030

Source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/transport-and-infrastructure/dubai-marine-transport-master-plan

Launched in 2023, Dubai Marine Transport Master Plan aims to enhance Dubai’s marine transport system through 2030.
Objectives
By 2030, the plan aims to achieve the following:
increase the number of marine transport users by 51 per cent, from 14.7 million (as of 2023) to 22.2 million
expand the total length of the marine transport network by 188 per cent, from 55 km (as of 2023) to 158 km
increase marine transport stations from 48 to 79 stations, placed along the Dubai Creek, Dubai Water Canal, the Arabian Gulf coastline and various waterfront projects
increase the number of passenger transport lines from 7 to 35
increase the marine transport fleet by 32 per cent, from 196 to 258 marine vessels
manufacture the world's first electric abra, using 3D printing technology. This initiative will reduce manufacturing time by 90 per cent and reduce production cost by 30 per cent.
