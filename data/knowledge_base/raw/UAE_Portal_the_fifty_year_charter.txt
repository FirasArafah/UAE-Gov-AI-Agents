The Fifty-Year Charter

Source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/innovation-and-future-shaping/the-fifty-year-charter

The Fifty-Year Charter was declared by H. H. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Vice President and Prime Minister of the UAE and Ruler of Dubai on 6 January 2019, the day he completed 50 years of service to the nation.
The Charter includes nine articles which aim to shape the future of Dubai, develop its community, enhance its quality of life and ensure a good future for generations to come through the following projects and programmes:
Dubai Silk Road
a geo-economic map for Dubai
setting up the first virtual commercial city
a central education file for every citizen
a doctor for every citizen
free economic and creative zones in universities
self-sufficiency in Dubai homes
cooperative companies in various sectors
annual growth in philanthropy.
