Dubai Food Security Strategy

Source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/environment-and-energy/food-security-strategy

Dubai’s Food Security Strategy is aligned with the National Food Security Strategy and aims to:
diversify sources of food import
enhance local production
reduce food wastage
enhance food safety and quality and
build the emirate’s preparedness to face crises.
The strategy classifies food products on their
nutritional content
importance of the product for consumers
level of demand and
whether they are irreplaceable and are used as raw material for other products.
The strategy depends on Dubai’s strong infrastructure and position as a strategic global logistics hub for its success.
