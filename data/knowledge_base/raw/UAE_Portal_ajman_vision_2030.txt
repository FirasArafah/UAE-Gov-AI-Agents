Ajman Vision 2030
Ajman Vision 2030 is a comprehensive plan that outlines the emirate's goals for the next decade. The vision is based on 8 guiding principles, which are:
Spirit of the Union: Union Upholding the UAE’s shared vision of building a strong, prosperous, and unified nation based on common values, principles, and heritage, strengthening national identity, unity, and cohesion
Cooperation: Working together with stakeholders from various sectors to achieve objectives, promoting joint problem-solving, co-design and collective action for more impactful outcomes
People Centricity: Adopting a 'people first' approach in designing, delivering, implementing, and evaluating public policies and services, prioritising the needs and voices of people to improve community outcomes
Sustainability: Balancing economic, social, and environmental considerations to ensure sustainable development that meets the needs of present and future generations, promoting long-term holistic wellbeing
Inclusivity: Ensuring equal access to opportunities, services, and resources for all residents, including women, children, youth, People of Determination, and the elderly, fostering social cohesion and diversity
Future Readiness: Proactively preparing for change and uncertainty by continuously adapting and innovating to build resilience and readiness to address future challenges and opportunities
Agility: Being responsive and adaptable to changing circumstances and emerging trends, empowering the economy, society, and Government to thrive in an ever-changing landscape
Accountability: Taking responsibility for actions and being answerable for the planning, reporting, and justifying of those actions to foster trust and transparency and achieve goals.
Ajman Vision 2030 has identified a set of strategic directives and objectives. They are:
Foster a competitive business environment and investment climate that drives economic development
Create a business-friendly environment and enhance investment promotion and facilitation
Improve the trade infrastructure and trade facilitation efforts
Foster a conducive environment for entrepreneurship, start-ups and the growth of SMEs
Enable increased private sector participation in public projects and services
Build human capital that can fulfil the Emirate’s aspirations
Ensure world-class education that drives educational attainment and readiness to shape the future
Foster research, development and innovation across the Emirate
Enhance workforce skills and promote lifelong learning
Enhance the attractiveness and livability of the Emirate
Enhance the performance of city operations and public services
Create active, vibrant urban centers that support thriving communities
Improve access to quality green and blue spaces, sporting, recreation, and community facilities
Promote good health, safety, security, and wellbeing
Establish Ajman as a hub for arts and culture
Protect, showcase, and develop Ajman’s cultural heritage
Support community engagement in art and cultural activities
Support the development of the art and culture industries
Deliver integrated and sustainable mobility
Enhance the quality and connectivity of road infrastructure
Enhance the quality and connectivity of the public transport network
Promote active transport through the provision of safe, accessible and enjoyable places for people to walk and cycle
Improve environmental sustainability
Achieve sustainable waste management and reduce pollution
Improve energy efficiency and increase use of alternative energy
Protect bio-diversity and conserve natural resources
Foster an inclusive, cohesive and empowered society
Ensure that the social services system is efficient, empowering and fair
Foster gender equality and empowerment of women, children, youth, people of determination and the elderly
Foster a cohesive society, whilst preserving the Emirati national identity
Enable the private sector, civil society and citizens to increase their contribution to the social sector agenda
Become a pioneering government that is agile, innovative, efficient and outcome-focused
Increase agility, innovation and efficiency within the Government’s structure, capabilities and culture
Ensure future-oriented plans, policies, laws and regulations
Enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of Government services
Ensure effective and efficient management of physical and digital assets
Improve financial performance and increase results orientation through outcome-based budgeting and performance measurement
Improve stakeholder engagement, access to information and community participation in decision-making
The vision's goals include:
building a comprehensive, cohesive and empowered society by empowering the social services system and its effectiveness
promoting gender equality, empowering women, children, youth, People of Determination and the elderly
preserving the Emirati national identity
enabling the contribution of the private sector and society to the social sector agenda
Improving environmental sustainability by achieving sustainable waste management, reducing pollution, improving energy efficiency, increasing the use of alternative energy, protecting biodiversity and preserving natural resources.
