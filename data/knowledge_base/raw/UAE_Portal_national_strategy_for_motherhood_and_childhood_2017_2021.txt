The National Strategy for Motherhood and Childhood 2017-2021

Source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/strategies-plans-and-visions-untill-2021/national-strategy-for-motherhood-and-childhood-2017-2021

The UAE Cabinet approved the National Strategy for Motherhood and Childhood 2017-2021 (PDF, 2.93 MB, Arabic) as a key reference for decision-makers in the childhood sector in the UAE. By launching this strategy for five years (2017-2021), the UAE emphasises on the UAE’s commitment to protect children’s rights.
The National Strategy for Motherhood and Childhood covers five strategic goals. They are:
enhancing the right of children and mothers to get comprehensive healthcare in a sustainable health environment
enhancing child protection under an integrated comprehensive system
enhancing child rights to good quality education, which develops their personalities and mental and physical capabilities
supporting effective participation of children in different aspects of life
developing policies and programmes based on evidences and accurate information to protect child rights.
