Government Communication Strategy 2017-2021

Source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/strategies-plans-and-visions-untill-2021/government-communication-strategy-2017-2021

In October 2017, the UAE Cabinet adopted the Government Communication Strategy 2017-2021. The strategy includes a set of strategic objectives and national priorities to achieve optimal communication system for federal entities.
The five-year communication strategy will adopt ideas and initiatives to advance the two-way communication channel between the government and the society, which will in turn enhance the overall government performance and decision-making.
The success of the strategy will depend on government entities, the private sector, media outlets, the general public and social media users.
The strategy aims to:
support the objectives of National Agenda and Vision 2021
enhance communication between the government and all sectors
lead an effective management of the government's image on various communication platforms
instil patriotic values
enhance ties with media outlets.
