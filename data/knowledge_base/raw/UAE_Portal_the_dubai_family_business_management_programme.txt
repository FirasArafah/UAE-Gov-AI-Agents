The Dubai Family Business Management Programme

Source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/finance-and-economy/the-dubai-family-business-management-programme

The Dubai Family Business Management Programme is an extension of a series of programmes and plans aimed at supporting family businesses, whose success indicates the growth of Dubai’s business community. It aims to nurture innovative minds capable of facing future challenges, envisioning the future and making it a reality. It adopts the best international practices for training and equipping leaders with local and international skill sets.
The programme is developed in affiliation with a globally renowned educational institution. It targets the second tier of succession in the leadership of Dubai-based family businesses. The primary objective is to equip the next generation of owners with the specialised knowledge and unique leadership skills necessary to lead a family business effectively.
The key objectives of the programme include:
enhancing governance and sustainability systems for family businesses
developing strategies and investments related to the movement of global trade
educating family businesses on local and international laws and legislation
promoting a culture of social responsibility among family businesses
building a local and international network of leaders, businesspeople and organisations
developing and providing tools and training to enhance media presence
preparing a new generation of family business leaders.
