Umm Al Quwain Vision 2033
Umm Al Quwain Vision 2033 (PDF, 500KB) supports the competitive capabilities of the emirate of Umm Al Quwain and places it on the path to the future and in the ranks of leadership. The emirate aims to do this by setting strategic policies and programmes that ensure the success of the development process in a sustainable and integrated framework with the Spirit of the Union and the UAE Centennial 2071.
Umm Al Quwain Vision 2033
Umm Al Quwain Vision 2033 was launched under the slogan, ‘High quality of life for a cohesive society built on a sustainable economy and attractive tourism enhanced by a digital government’ with
4 basic principles
4 main pillars
16 government objectives.
Principles of Umm Al Quwain Vision 2033
Umm Al Quwain Vision 2033 is based on four principles. They are:
Citizen first: harnessing all government capabilities and abilities to build a society in which citizens and residents enjoy quality of life, prosperity, security and safety, and receive all adequate health, sports and recreational services and facilities.
Corporate partnership: building institutional and community partnerships with all government bodies and institutions for strengthening the union.
Supreme economic interest: supporting the country’s progress towards achieving an active and competitive economy with a focus on creating an attractive tourism environment.
Human capital: focus on human resources as the primary driver of a sustainable future for the emirate.
The main pillars of Umm Al Quwain Vision 2033 are:
quality of life
sustainable competitive economy
attractive tourism
digital government.
