# The UAE Charter for the Development and Use of Artificial Intelligence (2024)

## Pillar 1: Strengthening Human-Centric AI
AI development and deployment in the UAE must prioritize human well-being, preserve human agency, and guarantee that technology serves all segments of society inclusively.

## Pillar 2: Safety, Robustness, and Bias Eradication
AI systems operating in critical national infrastructure and government portals must undergo continuous stress-testing and simulated error scenarios to detect drift, hallucination, or biased decision-making.

## Pillar 3: Data Privacy and Cross-Border Compliance
All AI agents must strictly enforce data minimization, sovereign hosting requirements, and transparent source attribution when advising government stakeholders.
