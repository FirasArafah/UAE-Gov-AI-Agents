# UAE AI Ethics Principles and Guidelines

## Principle 1: Fairness and Non-Discrimination (Algorithmic Bias Mitigation)
AI systems deployed in the United Arab Emirates—especially within government services, residency allocation, and public administration—must treat all individuals equitably.
1. **Disparate Impact Standard:** AI models must be audited regularly to ensure approval rates across demographic groups (nationality, gender, age, socioeconomic tier) do not exhibit statistically significant disparate impact (Four-Fifths Rule / 80% threshold).
2. **Dataset Representativeness:** Training data used for screening algorithms must be representative and purged of historical biases or proxy variables that correlate with protected characteristics.

## Principle 2: Transparency and Explainability
1. Government entities utilizing AI for high-stakes decisions (including Golden Residency and Visa issuance) must provide clear, auditable chain-of-thought logs explaining why an application was flagged, approved, or rejected.
2. Applicants have the right to know when an AI agent is assessing their profile.

## Principle 3: Human Oversight and Accountability (Human-in-the-Loop)
1. High-risk government AI systems must never operate as fully autonomous black boxes for final rejections.
2. Any rejection or adverse flag generated by an AI screening model must be routed to a qualified human officer for review before a final administrative decision is issued.
