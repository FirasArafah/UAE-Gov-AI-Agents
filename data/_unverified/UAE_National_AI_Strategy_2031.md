# UAE National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence 2031

## Strategic Objective 3: Develop a Fertile Ecosystem for AI
To position the UAE as a global testbed for AI governance and ethical deployment across public services, healthcare, and telecommunications.

## Strategic Objective 6: Ensure Strong Governance and Effective Regulation
1. Establish the UAE AI Council to oversee cross-ministry coordination between the ICP, MOHRE, TDRA, and the UAE Data Office.
2. Mandate standardized audit protocols aligned with international telecommunication and AI standards (including ITU-T Y.3172 architectural pipelines) to ensure AI resilience, auditability, and zero tolerance for algorithmic bias in smart government services.
