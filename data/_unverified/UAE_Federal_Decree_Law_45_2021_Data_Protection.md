# UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL)

## Article 4: General Principles of Personal Data Protection
1. Processing of Personal Data must be made in a fair, transparent, and lawful manner.
2. Personal Data must be collected for a specific and clear purpose, and may not be processed at any subsequent time in a manner incompatible with that purpose.

## Article 18: Right to Object to Automated Processing (Algorithmic Screening)
1. The Data Subject shall have the right to object to and stop any decision based solely on Automated Processing, including Profiling, which produces legal consequences or seriously affects the Data Subject (such as automated visa or residency screening).
2. Automated systems used in public service eligibility must not rely on sensitive personal data to create discriminatory outcomes unless explicitly authorized by federal law with human-in-the-loop oversight.

## Article 20: Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)
1. The Controller shall, prior to undertaking any Processing that uses modern technologies or automated profiling on a large scale, carry out an assessment of the impact of the proposed Processing operations on the protection of Personal Data and fundamental fairness.
