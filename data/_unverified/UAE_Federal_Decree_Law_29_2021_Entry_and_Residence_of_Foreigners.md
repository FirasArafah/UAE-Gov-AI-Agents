# Federal Decree-Law No. 29 of 2021 Regarding Entry and Residence of Foreigners

## Article 5: Competence of the Federal Authority (ICP)
The Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security (ICP) is solely responsible for issuing, renewing, and canceling entry visas and residence permits in accordance with the Executive Regulations.

## Article 9: Objective Criteria for Visa Assessment
1. Entry visas and residence permits shall be evaluated strictly based on statutory eligibility criteria: valid travel documentation, security clearance, financial solvency or employment contract, and health fitness.
2. Administrative or automated screening mechanisms may not introduce arbitrary demographic penalties or unapproved scoring weights outside the parameters established by the Cabinet.
