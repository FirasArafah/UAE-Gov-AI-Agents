# UAE Cabinet Resolution No. 65 of 2022: Golden Residency Eligibility Criteria

## Section 1: Investors in Public Investments or Real Estate
1. Real estate investors are eligible for a 10-year Golden Residency upon purchasing property valued at no less than AED 2,000,000.
2. Nationality or country of origin shall not alter the financial threshold required under this article.

## Section 2: Skilled Professionals and Exceptional Talents
1. Skilled professionals must hold a valid employment contract in the UAE, be classified in the first or second occupational level by MOHRE, hold a minimum of a bachelor's degree, and earn a minimum monthly salary of AED 30,000.
2. Exceptional talents in AI, Data Science, Healthcare, and Culture are evaluated on merit, patents, or endorsement by a competent federal/local authority, independent of demographic background or gender.
3. **Algorithmic Screening Compliance:** Any automated pre-screening tool evaluating Golden Visa applicants must verify degree equivalency and salary thresholds objectively without penalizing applicants based on university region or nationality.
